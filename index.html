a little context

Why I made this site

First & foremost:
I want to create really meaningful friendships!
(and maybe find a partner along the way...)

I'm calling these intentional friendships: relationships that help each other become the best versions of ourselves, built on shared values, shared goals, and shared care.

Don't get me wrong, I love a good time! Play, fun, and adventure are super important to me. But I am looking for the fun we have together to be in service of helping us grow:

  • What can we create together?
  • How can we support each other's flourishing?
  • Who do we want to be, and how can we bring that out in one another?
  • (and most importantly)
    How can the things we do and make together help make the world a better place?

And if somewhere along the way, one of those intentional friendships grows into a partnership, that's wonderful too ❤️

here's a bit more detail of what I'm looking for:

Intentional Friendships

Okay, here's my intentional friendship pitch:

  1. Let's confirm we're on the same page. Do you care about the world around you? Do you want to help make it more regenerative, cooperative, creative, and caring? If yes, amazing, we are simpatico.
  2. Let's share goals 🙂 What are you working on? Who are you trying to become? What are you dreaming of, experimenting with, questioning?
  3. Let's see how we can support each other on those goals! Maybe it's working out together or texting to make sure you went to bed on time. Maybe it's collaborating on a project or teaching each other skills. Maybe it's emotional support or co-working. It can be whatever! The goal is to be genuinely invested in each other's growth, hearts, minds, and spirits.

Check out My Goals

Outside of goals, I think the other important aspect of intentional friendship is that we can be our full, authentic selves with each other.

So yes, let's have fun together!
But can we also:

  • Be weird, ugly, dorky, and vulnerable together?
  • Hold each other when we're depressed?
  • Inspire each other when we feel lost?
  • Experiment and play together?
  • Make mistakes and learn together?
  • Grieve and cry and laugh together?

Basically, can we do life together?

If we can say things like:

  • “I like who I am / who I am becoming when I am with you”
  • “You bring out the best in me”
  • “You feel like home”
  • “I feel more alive and whole with you in my life”
  • “I’m so proud of what we are creating together”

I think that’s the mark of really wonderful intentional friendships <3

Lastly, I'll leave you with two quotes that have been resonating with me recently:

Community is much more than belonging to something; it's about doing something together that makes belonging matter.
Some people think they are in community, but they are only in proximity. True community requires commitment and openness. It is a willingness to extend yourself to encounter and know the other.
Partnership

What I am looking for in a partner is exactly what I’m looking for in my friendships (see intentional friendships above) with the added layer of romantic love and affection.

In the short term,

I am looking for someone who would like to help each other reach our goals in a nurturing way.
I think some fun, inspiration, and tenderness could feel really nourishing in this stage of my life <3

A note here: if you look at My Goals, you'll see I am in the process of finding a new career. So it's important to me that whoever I'm romantically involved with at this time is understanding and in support of that goal, and whatever boundaries I might have to make with my time and attention to achieve it.

That said, it's not just about me. I love helping my partner achieve their goals too! So the support flows both ways.

Whether this connection lasts forever or only for a time is alright... So long as we helped each other towards our goals and made each other feel loved, that is a worthwhile connection to me.

In the long term,

I would love to find “my person”
My teammate in life
Someone I can dream and build with til we're old and grey as they say

But let's start with intentional friends and see where things go 😉
